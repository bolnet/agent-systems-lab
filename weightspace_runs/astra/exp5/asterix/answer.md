I use a **fixed-column interception policy**, keeping the player at column 5 and moving vertically to meet incoming treasure. This sacrifices horizontal chasing for simpler, more reliable collision avoidance.

The first layer contains eight direction-sensitive enemy-risk features, eight player-row indicators, and sixteen treasure features gated by the player’s position above or below each row. Treasure moving away from column 5 is ignored. The second layer computes 22 action-specific safety costs, two treasure-pursuit signals, and eight bonuses for waiting in an incoming treasure’s row. Neighboring-row penalties provide a buffer against sticky actions. Safety receives much larger weights than pursuit.
